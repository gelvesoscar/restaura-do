The brand's action element: fully rounded pill, warm shadow on the primary, soft 0.985 press scale.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg">Empezar mi rutina</Button>
<Button variant="ghost">Leer la ciencia <Icon name="arrow-right" size="sm" /></Button>
```

- One primary per view; `secondary` (verde pulpa) for the second-strongest action.
- Labels are verbs in infinitive Spanish, sentence case, no exclamation marks.
